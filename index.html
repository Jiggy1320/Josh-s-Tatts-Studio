FAQ

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. Deposits are strictly non-refundable. It reserves your slot, covers drawing time, and protects my schedule. If you no-show, the money stays with me.

Does it hurt?

It is a needle repeatedly dragging ink through your epidermis. Yes, it hurts. You will survive. Eat a heavy meal and hydrate before you sit in the chair.
